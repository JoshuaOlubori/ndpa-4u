As this Act takes effect, references to the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau will now be read as references to the newly established Commission. All staff previously with the Bureau will continue in their roles under terms no less favorable than before. Existing agreements, contracts, records, and equipment of the Bureau are now officially transitioned to the Commission, and its assets are now vested in the Commission.

Legal proceedings or causes of action involving the Bureau can be continued by or against the Commission. Importantly, all existing orders, regulations, licenses, and other official documents issued by the Bureau or the National Information Technology Development Agency remain in effect until they expire or are replaced by the Commission.
