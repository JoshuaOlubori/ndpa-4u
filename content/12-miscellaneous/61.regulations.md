The Commission has the authority to create regulations to achieve the objectives of this Act. This includes managing its financial affairs, protecting personal data and data subjects, and defining how it will exercise its powers and perform its functions. Regulations may also prescribe application forms, complaint procedures, and the frequency and content of compliance returns for organizations of major importance.

Furthermore, these regulations can set fees, fines, and charges, and address any other matters the Commission deems necessary for the Act's success. The Commission can also establish offences for violating these regulations, with penalties not exceeding those defined in the Act itself. Before finalized, draft regulations will be published on the Commission's website for public comment.
