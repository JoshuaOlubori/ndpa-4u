To obtain evidence for an investigation, the Commission can apply to a Judge in Chambers for a warrant. A Judge may issue this warrant if they are satisfied that someone has contravened the Act, or is likely to do so, or if it is necessary to prevent an offence, investigate a data breach, or secure electronic evidence.

A warrant authorizes the Commission, alongside a law enforcement officer, to enter and search premises or vehicles where an offence is suspected to be occurring or where evidence exists. They can also stop and search persons on the premises and seize, seal, or detain any evidence of a violation.

Furthermore, the Commission is empowered to search computer systems and networks, use technology to decode or decrypt data into a readable format, and require individuals conversant with electronic devices to produce them for the investigation.
