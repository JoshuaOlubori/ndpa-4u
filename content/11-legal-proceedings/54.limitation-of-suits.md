Legal action cannot be brought against the Commission, its Council members, or its staff for actions taken in the execution of the Act or public duties unless the suit is filed within three months of the event in question. In cases of ongoing damage or injury, the suit must be initiated within three months of the harm ceasing.

Before starting any legal proceedings, the intending plaintiff or their agent must provide the Commission, member, or staff with at least one month's written notice. This notice must clearly explain the cause of action, the specifics of the claim, the plaintiff's address, and the relief being sought.

Additionally, suits against officials or employees of the Commission are subject to the provisions of the Public Officers Protection Act, in accordance with the terms of this Act.
