The National Commissioner, Council members, Commission staff, and other authorized personnel will be indemnified using the Commission's assets for losses, claims, and liabilities incurred while performing their official duties. This indemnity also covers legal expenses for defending civil or criminal proceedings, provided the individual is acquitted, the case is decided in their favor, or the proceedings are dismissed without a finding of duty breach. Indemnity also applies if a court grants relief from liability for negligence, default, or breach of trust.
