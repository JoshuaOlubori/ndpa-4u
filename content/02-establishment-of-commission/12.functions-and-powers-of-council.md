The functions of the Council are to formulate and provide overall policy direction for the affairs of the Commission. It approves strategic plans, action plans, budget support programmes, annual reports, and financial reports submitted by the National Commissioner.

The Council approves the terms and conditions of service for the employees of the Commission, including remuneration, allowances, and pension benefits. It also approves staff regulations for appointment, promotion, and discipline.

Furthermore, the Council provides advice and counsel to the National Commissioner and assists in matters relating to compliance by ministries, departments, and agencies of government. The Council has the power to delegate any of its functions to a committee set up in accordance with the provisions of this Act.
