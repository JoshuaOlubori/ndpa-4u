A member of the Council ceases to be a member if they resign their appointment by giving notice in writing to the President through the Minister. Membership also ceases if the member becomes of unsound mind, is bankrupt, or is convicted of a felony or any offence involving dishonesty or fraud.

Furthermore, a member may be removed if they become incapable of performing their duties due to physical or mental illness, or if they are found guilty of serious misconduct. Continuous absence from three consecutive meetings without a good reason is also a ground for removal.

The President may remove a member of the Council if it is in the public interest to do so. In the event of a vacancy, the President shall appoint a new member to fill the vacancy for the remainder of the term, following the same procedure used for the original appointment.
