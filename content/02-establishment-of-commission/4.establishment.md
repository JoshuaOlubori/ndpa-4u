There is established the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. The Commission is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal. It may sue or be sued in its corporate name, and it may acquire, hold and dispose of its property.

The Commission shall have its head office in the Federal Capital Territory. It may maintain other offices in any part of Nigeria for the purposes of achieving its objects. Subject to the approval of the Council, the National Commissioner may acquire other offices and premises for the use of the Commission.
