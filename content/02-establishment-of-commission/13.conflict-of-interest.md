A member of the Council shall ensure that personal interests do not conflict with their duties under this Act. They shall not make secret profit in the course of discharging official duties and must fully disclose any personal, commercial, financial, or other interest connected with the business of the Commission.

A member with a conflict of interest is ineligible to participate in any Council deliberation or voting related to that matter. They must also not accept any gift or advantage for anything done or likely to be done with respect to the responsibilities of the Council.

Contravention of the provisions regarding secret profit or accepting gifts is an offence. On conviction, a member is liable to a fine of at least N10,000,000 or imprisonment for up to three years, or both. For other violations regarding participation in deliberations, the penalty is a fine of at least N5,000,000 or imprisonment for up to two years, or both.
