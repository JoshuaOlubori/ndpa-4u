The Commission regulates the deployment of technological and organisational measures to enhance personal data protection. It fosters the development of personal data protection technologies in accordance with recognised international best practices and applicable international law.

Where necessary, the Commission accredit, license, and register suitable persons to provide data protection compliance services. It also registers data controllers and data processors of major importance.

The Commission promotes awareness on the obligation of data controllers and data processors under this Act, as well as public awareness and understanding of personal data protection, rights, and obligations. It handles complaints relating to violations of the Act or subsidiary legislation.

Furthermore, the Commission collaborates with relevant ministries, agencies, and persons to attain the objectives of the Act. It ensures compliance with national and international personal data protection obligations and best practices, participates in international fora, and determines the adequacy of data protection standards for cross-border transfers.

The Commission also collects and publishes information regarding data protection and personal data breaches. It advises the government on policy issues, submits legislative proposals for strengthening data protection, and carries out other legal actions necessary for its functions.
