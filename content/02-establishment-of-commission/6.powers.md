The Commission has the power to do everything necessary or convenient for the performance of its functions. Specifically, it can issue and publish regulations, rules, guidelines, and codes of conduct.

The Commission has the authority to summon and examine witnesses, require the production of documents, and conduct inspections. It can issue compliance and enforcement orders, and impose penalties for violations of the Act or its regulations.

Additionally, the Commission can prescribe fees, establish and maintain registers, and enter into contracts. It also has the power to acquire and dispose of property and perform any other acts required to give effect to its functions.
