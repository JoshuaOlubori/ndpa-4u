Personal data should not be transferred from Nigeria to another country unless the recipient is subject to laws, binding corporate rules, or standard contractual clauses that ensure an adequate level of data protection. Alternatively, a transfer may occur if one of the specific conditions defined in the Act applies.

Data controllers and processors must record the basis for any such transfer and the evidence of adequate protection. The Commission may issue regulations requiring these parties to notify it about the measures in place and explain their adequacy.

Furthermore, the Commission has the power to designate specific categories of personal data that may be subject to additional restrictions on cross-border transfer, based on the nature of the data and the potential risks involved for data subjects.
