If an adequacy determination is absent, personal data can only be transferred if the data subject has consented after being informed of the potential risks. Transfers are also allowed if necessary for a contract's performance or at the subject's request before a contract is finalized.

A transfer is permitted if it is for the sole benefit of a data subject and obtaining consent is not practicable, but would likely have been given. Other permissible grounds include important reasons of public interest, the establishment or defense of legal claims, or protecting the vital interests of a person who is incapable of giving consent.

Finally, any international or multi-national codes or certification mechanisms adopted as a standard for data protection or sovereignty in Nigeria must be approved by the National Assembly.
