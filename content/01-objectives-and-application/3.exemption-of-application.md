This Act does not apply to the processing of personal data carried out by one or more persons solely for personal or household purposes, provided that such processing does not constitute a violation of the fundamental right to privacy of a data subject.

Subject to constitutional rights and freedoms, the obligations under Part V, other than sections 24, 25, 32, and 40, do not apply to a data controller or data processor if the processing is carried out by a competent authority for the prevention, investigation, detection, prosecution, or adjudication of a criminal offence or execution of a criminal penalty.

Exemptions also apply to processing carried out by a competent authority for the prevention or control of a national public health emergency, or as necessary for national security.

In addition, processing in respect of publication in the public interest for journalism, educational, artistic, and literary purposes is exempt to the extent that obligations and rights are incompatible with such purposes. Processing necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims is also exempt.

The Commission may by regulation prescribe types of personal data and processing that may be exempted from this Act. Notwithstanding these exemptions, the Commission may issue guidance notices containing legal safeguards and best practices where processing is likely to violate the core principles of sections 24 and 25.
