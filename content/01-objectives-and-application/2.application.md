This Act applies to the processing of personal data, whether by automated means or not.

The Act applies where the data controller or data processor is domiciled in, resident in, or operating in Nigeria. It also applies where the processing of personal data occurs within Nigeria.

Furthermore, the Act applies where the data controller or data processor is not domiciled in, resident in, or operating in Nigeria, but is processing personal data of a data subject in Nigeria.
