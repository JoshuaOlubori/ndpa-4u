Data controllers and processors must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security, integrity, and confidentiality of personal data. These measures should protect against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, misuse, alteration, and unauthorized disclosure or access.

When determining these measures, factors such as the amount and sensitivity of the data, the potential for harm to data subjects, the extent of processing, and the duration of data retention must be considered. The availability and cost of current technology relative to the size of the organization are also important considerations.

Effective measures may include pseudonymization, encryption, and systems that ensure the ongoing resilience of processing operations. Data controllers should also have processes for restoring data access after incidents, conducting regular risk assessments, and testing the effectiveness of security measures against evolving threats.
