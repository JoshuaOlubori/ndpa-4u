
## SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS RELATING TO PROCEEDINGS OF THE COUNCIL

### Council to Regulate Proceedings
The Council has the authority to make standing orders regulating its own proceedings and to set up any necessary committees. It is required to meet at least once every quarter of the year.

### Presiding Officer
Every meeting of the Council will be led by the Chairman. In the Chairman's absence, the members present will elect one of their own to preside over the meeting.

### Quorum
A quorum for a Council meeting consists of the Chairman (or the person presiding) and four other members. The quorum for any committee established by the Council will be determined by the Council itself.

### Voting
Each member present at a meeting is entitled to one vote. Decisions are made by a majority vote of those present and voting. In the event of a tie, the Chairman or the presiding member has a casting vote. The Council may also invite experts for advice on specific matters, but these individuals cannot vote and do not count toward a quorum.

### Teleconference meeting
The Council can hold meetings through teleconferencing or any other communication method that allows all participants to hear and be heard simultaneously. Members participating via teleconference are considered present for all purposes. The Council may establish specific procedures for recording these meetings in its official minutes.

### Committees of the Council
The Council can appoint standing or ad-hoc committees to report on any matter within its concern. Each committee must be led by a Council member and will consist of a number of people determined by the Council. Decisions made by a committee only take effect once they are approved or ratified by the Council.

### Seal of the Commission
The seal of the Commission must be authenticated by the signature of the National Commissioner or another member authorized by the Council. Contracts or instruments that do not require a seal can be executed by the National Commissioner or a specifically authorized staff member. Any document appearing to be signed or sealed on behalf of the Commission will be accepted as evidence unless proven otherwise.

### Miscellaneous
The proceedings of the Council or its committees remain valid even if there is a vacancy in membership, a defect in an appointment, or if an unauthorized person participated.

Members of the Council or its committees must disclose any personal interest in a contract or arrangement being considered by the Commission. They must disclose this interest in advance, refrain from influencing the decision, take no part in the discussion, and be absent from that part of the meeting. All such disclosures must be recorded in the official minutes.
