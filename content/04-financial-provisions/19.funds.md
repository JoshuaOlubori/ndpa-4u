The Commission shall establish a Fund for the performance of its functions. This Fund will be populated by take-off grants and other money appropriated by the National Assembly, as well as fees, levies, and other charges collected by the Commission.

Additionally, the Fund will include all other sums of money that may accrue to the Commission through gifts, testamentary dispositions, and endowments. It will also receive any other money from various sources, including loans borrowed in accordance with the Act.
