The proceeds of the Fund shall be applied to several areas, including the cost of administration of the Commission. This includes the payment of salaries, allowances, and other benefits for the National Commissioner, members of the Council, and employees.

The Fund also covers the payment of pensions and other retirement benefits. Additionally, it is used for the maintenance of property vested in or acquired by the Commission, and any other expenditure related to the functions of the Commission under this Act.
