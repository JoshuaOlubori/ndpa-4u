The Commission must keep proper accounts and records of its receipts, payments, assets, and liabilities. At the end of each financial year, it shall prepare a statement of accounts in a form approved by the Council.

Within six months after the end of each financial year, the Commission shall submit its accounts for auditing. The auditors must be appointed from the list and in accordance with the guidelines provided by the Auditor-General for the Federation.
