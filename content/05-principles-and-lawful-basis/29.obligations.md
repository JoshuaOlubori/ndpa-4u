When a data controller engages a data processor, or a processor engages another, the engaging party must ensure the engaged processor complies with the Act's principles and obligations.

The engaged processor must assist the engaging party in fulfilling obligations to honor data subjects' rights and must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure data security, integrity, and confidentiality.

Furthermore, the processor must provide necessary information to demonstrate compliance and notify the engaging party when a new processor is engaged. These measures must be backed by a written agreement between the parties involved.
