If data processing is likely to result in a high risk to a data subject's rights and freedoms due to its nature or scope, the data controller must conduct a data privacy impact assessment beforehand. If this assessment still indicates high risks despite planned measures, the controller must consult the Commission.

The Commission may issue regulations or directives regarding these assessments, including which categories of processing or persons are subject to the requirement.

A data privacy impact assessment is a process to identify risks and impacts. it includes a systematic description of the processing and its purpose, an assessment of the necessity and proportionality of the processing, an evaluation of risks to rights and freedoms, and the measures planned to address those risks and safeguard the data.
