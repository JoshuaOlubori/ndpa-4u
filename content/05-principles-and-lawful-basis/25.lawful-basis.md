Data processing is lawful if the data subject has given and not withdrawn consent for specific purposes. It is also lawful if the processing is necessary for the performance of a contract involving the data subject or to take steps at their request before entering a contract.

Processing is also permitted for compliance with a legal obligation, to protect the vital interests of the data subject or another person, or for a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority.

Furthermore, processing is lawful if it is necessary for the legitimate interests pursued by the data controller, processor, or a third party. However, these interests are not considered legitimate if they override the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject, are incompatible with other lawful bases, or if the data subject would not reasonably expect such processing.
