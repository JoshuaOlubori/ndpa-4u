For data subjects who are children or lack the legal capacity to consent, the data controller must obtain the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Appropriate mechanisms, such as government-approved identification, must be used to verify age and consent.

This requirement does not apply if the processing is necessary to protect the subject's vital interests, is for education or medical care under a duty of confidentiality, or is required for legal proceedings.

The Commission will create regulations for processing data of children aged 13 and above in relation to electronic services. Importantly, all data processing involving children must remain consistent with the provisions of the Child’s Right Act.
