Before collecting personal data directly from a data subject, a data controller must provide information about their identity, residence, and communication means. They must also explain the lawful basis and purposes of the processing, as well as the recipients of the data.

Data subjects must be informed of their rights, the retention period for their data, and their right to lodge a complaint with the Commission. If automated decision-making or profiling is used, its significance and consequences must be explained, along with the right to object.

If data is collected indirectly, the same information must be provided unless the data subject already has it or providing it is impossible or requires disproportionate effort. All of this information should be in a clear, concise, and accessible privacy policy tailored to the target audience.
