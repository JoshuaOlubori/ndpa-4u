Data controllers and processors of major importance must register with the Commission within six months of the Act's commencement or upon reaching that status. This registration involves providing the Commission with their names, addresses, and details of their data protection officer.

They must also describe the types of personal data they process, the estimated number of data subjects, and the purposes for the processing. Information about typical data recipients, cross-border transfer plans, and an overview of security measures and safeguards must also be provided.

Any significant changes to this information must be reported to the Commission within 60 days. The Commission will maintain and publish an online register of these organizations. An organization can be removed from the register if it stops functioning as a entity of major importance. The Commission also has the power to exempt certain classes of organizations if registration is deemed unnecessary or disproportionate.
