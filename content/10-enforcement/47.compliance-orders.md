If the Commission finds that a data controller or processor has violated or is likely to violate the Act, it may issue a compliance order. This order could take the form of a warning about potential violations, a requirement to comply with specific provisions (including honoring data subject rights), or a cease and desist order to stop prohibited data processing.

Every compliance order must be in writing. It must clearly state the specific provisions of the Act that were violated, the measures required to remedy the situation, the deadline for implementation, and the right of the recipient to seek judicial review.
