Any data subject who feels aggrieved by a data controller's or processor's actions or inactions in violation of the Act can lodge a complaint with the Commission. The Commission will investigate these complaints provided they are not frivolous or vexatious. It can also initiate investigations on its own if it suspects a violation has occurred or is likely to happen.

During an investigation, the Commission has the power to order individuals to attend examinations, produce relevant documents or records, and provide written statements under oath. If the relevant material is stored electronically, the Commission can require access for inspection, and the information must be provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

The Commission may also act as a mediator, making representations between the complainant and the data controller or processor. To handle these responsibilities effectively, the Commission will establish a dedicated unit for complaints and investigations and adopt clear rules and procedures for their conduct.
