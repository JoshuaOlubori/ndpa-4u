Following an investigation, if the Commission determines a violation has occurred, it can issue an enforcement order or impose sanctions. This decision must be shared in writing with the data controller, processor, and any involved complainant. Enforcement orders can require a remedy for the violation, compensation for affected data subjects, or an accounting of profits gained from the violation.

The Commission may also impose a penalty or remedial fee. For organizations of major importance, the maximum penalty is either N10,000,000 or 2% of their annual gross revenue, whichever is higher. For other organizations, the maximum is N2,000,000 or 2% of their annual gross revenue.

In deciding on the appropriate sanction, the Commission considers the gravity and duration of the infringement, the purpose of the processing, and the number of subjects affected. Technical factors like the damage caused, mitigation efforts, whether the violation was intentional or negligent, and the degree of cooperation with the Commission are also taken into account.
