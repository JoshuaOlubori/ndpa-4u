Data subjects have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, if those decisions produce legal or similarly significant effects.

This right doesn't apply if the decision is necessary for a contract between the subject and the controller, is authorized by a written law with proper safeguards, or is based on the data subject's consent.

In cases where automated decisions are allowed, the data controller must still implement measures to protect the subject's rights. These include the right to obtain human intervention, the right to express their point of view, and the right to contest the decision.
