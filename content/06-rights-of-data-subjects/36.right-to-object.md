A data subject has the right to object to the processing of their personal data. Upon such an objection, the data controller must stop processing the data unless they can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or a public interest that overrides the data subject's fundamental rights and freedoms.

If personal data is being processed for direct marketing, including any related profiling, the data subject can object at any time. Once an objection to direct marketing is made, the personal data must no longer be used for those purposes.
