The Commission may establish regulations for personal data portability. This right entitles a data subject to receive their personal data from a data controller in a structured, machine-readable format and to transmit that data to another controller without hindrance. Where technically possible, the data should be transmitted directly between controllers.

The Commission may also define the specific conditions under which this right can be exercised and the obligations it places on data controllers or processors, including considerations for timing and costs.
