There shall be for the Commission a National Commissioner, appointed by the President on the recommendation of the Minister. The National Commissioner is the chief executive and accounting officer of the Commission, responsible for the execution of policies and administration of its affairs.

A person qualifies for appointment as National Commissioner if they possess at least 15 years’ cognate experience in data protection, computer science, law, information technology, or any other relevant field. They must also have the requisite professional qualifications for the office.

The National Commissioner holds office for a term of four years and may be eligible for re-appointment for one additional term of four years. The specific terms and conditions are specified in the letter of appointment.
